QR wayfinding for hospitals

Patients and visitors scan a code on the wall, choose where they want to go, and follow the route on their phone. No app and no hardware.

On this page

    The problem

    Hospitals grow in layers. A new wing is added, a department moves, a lift is closed. Visitors arrive once, so every trip is a first trip, and the front desk becomes the help line. Read more in our hospital wayfinding guide.

    How it works at your hospital

    1. You share a floor plan.
    2. We mark destinations, walkways, lifts and stairs.
    3. We publish a link and QR codes for the walls, at entrances, lifts and junctions.
    4. Visitors scan, choose a destination and follow the route, even between floors.

    Destinations visitors can choose

    Registration, OPD, consultation rooms, diagnostics and radiology, laboratory, pharmacy, billing, wards, ICU, lifts and stairs. The list is set by your hospital, so we only map what you have.

    What it means for your team

    • Visitors get another way to find the answer without asking at the desk.
    • When a department moves or a ward is renamed, the route is updated and the QR code stays the same.
    • The QR plaques sit next to your existing signboards. They add to them, they do not replace them.
