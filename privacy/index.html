Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information Areyos (“we”, “us”), based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, collects through areyos.in, the demo and the wayfinding service, and how we use it. We aim to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • When you contact us. If you email, call or message us, we receive what you send, such as your name, email address, phone number, organisation and message.
  • Website analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. It reports aggregate information such as pages viewed, referring site, country, browser and device type. It does not use cookies or track you across other sites.
  • Using a venue map. The map does not require an account. We designed it not to ask for your name or contact details. To give venues usage analytics, we may record anonymous events, such as which QR code was scanned, which destinations were searched and when. We do not use this to identify individual visitors, and venues receive aggregated results.
  • Venue content. Floor plans and details that venues give us to build their maps.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to you and discuss pilots, plans and support.
  • To build, run and improve venue maps and the Service.
  • To give venues aggregated analytics, depending on their plan.
  • To keep the site secure and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Service (for example hosting and analytics providers), with the venue you are visiting where the information is aggregated usage data, when the law requires it, or with your agreement. Providers act on our instructions.

4. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, then delete or anonymise it. Messages from prospective customers are kept while the conversation or business relationship is active.

5. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect information. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Under applicable law you may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, have it erased, or withdraw consent where we rely on it. You may also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. To do so, email anas@areyos.in. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

7. Children

The Service is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version.
