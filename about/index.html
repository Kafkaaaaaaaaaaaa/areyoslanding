About Areyos

Areyos is a Mumbai-based software company that helps people find their way inside large buildings. A venue shares its floor plan, and Areyos turns it into a digital map that opens from a printed QR code in any phone’s browser. There is no app to install, no hardware in the building and no personal data collected from visitors. Areyos is built for hospitals, malls, campuses, airports and event venues, and is starting in Mumbai, Chhattisgarh and Odisha. Areyos is a registered MSME (Udyam registration UDYAM-MH-18-0593247).

On this page

    Founder: Anas Khan

    Anas Khan is the founder of Areyos, based in Mumbai. He started Areyos to help visitors find hospital departments, shops and exhibition stalls without asking for directions at every corner. Anas leads product and partnerships.

    Why Areyos exists

    Large buildings grow in layers. Departments move, wings open and signs go out of date, so visitors stop reception staff to ask the way. Areyos adds a clear, printed QR code beside your existing signs, so a visitor can scan, choose a place and walk there.

    What we believe

    • Simple for the visitor. No app, no account, nothing personal collected.
    • Light for the venue. No beacons, no wiring. Printed plaques and a map.
    • Honest about what it is. QR codes give a known starting point and a clear route. They do not track people.
