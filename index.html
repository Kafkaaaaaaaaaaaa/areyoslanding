  1. Scan the code

    Point the phone's camera at the QR code on the wall or desk. The map opens in the browser. No app, no sign-up.

  2. Choose where to go

    Search for a place or tap a suggestion. The blue dot shows where the code was scanned.

  3. See the route first

    Before setting off, the visitor sees the walk time, the floors involved and the time they should arrive.

  4. Follow one step at a time

    Each instruction appears on screen. Tap Next after every turn, or Back to see one again. Scan the next code you pass to update your location.

  5. Arrive

    The screen confirms the visitor has arrived. From there they can finish, or find another place.
